Nexus Drift

Drop into procedurally generated caves, mine glowing deposits, and fight off swarms of geometric enemies. Everything is drawn from neon shapes.

PC (keyboard & mouse or controller). Not playable yet.

Working now

  • Shooting with an overheat meter
  • A mining beam that cuts through cave walls
  • Six enemy types, each with its own behaviour
  • Each cave is generated from a seed and gets harder the deeper you go
  • Clear the objective, step through the portal, choose an upgrade, and go again

Planned

  • The Nexus, a home base you build up between expeditions
  • Crafting modular weapons and gear
  • Co-op multiplayer
  • Chill or intense: you choose how much risk to take on
